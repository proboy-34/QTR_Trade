# QTR_Trade audit and correction pass — 2026-09-25

Scope: full repository audit against the brief, which says QTR must be a disciplined, evidence-based
crypto trader that may answer **NO TRADE**, with truthful data, AI and execution states. Only
Binance (primary), Google Gemini, Finnhub and FRED are in scope. Real-money trading stays disabled.

Legend: **REAL** = observed against the live provider in this run. **MOCKED** = deterministic
offline test with a fake HTTP transport. **NOT VERIFIED** = no real evidence either way.

## A. What exists

A FastAPI modular monolith (SQLAlchemy 2, Alembic 0001→0006, SQLite locally and PostgreSQL as the
target) with a React/TypeScript terminal. It includes:
- Binance public market data: history, WebSocket, multi-asset stream.
- Dynamic universe and eligibility, scanner, regime engine and market memory.
- Declarative strategy DSL with IS/OOS/walk-forward/robustness and deflated Sharpe.
- Hypothesis pipeline, strategy repository with human promotion, paper exchange, risk engine and
  persisted kill switches.
- Learning: trade memory, counterfactuals, decay and discrepancy.
- AI gateway with a claim firewall and scheduler jobs.
- Code added in this pass: provider verification, FRED point-in-time ingestion, evidence
  reasoner, Binance Spot Testnet adapter and venue separation.

## B. What matches the brief

- Decisions are deterministic, and NO TRADE/WAIT is a first-class outcome.
- The AI cannot create orders or facts. Its output is typed and verified by reference, and it
  cannot change rules.
- Promotion is always a human decision, and learning produces hypotheses rather than rule edits.
- Risk owns size, stop and target. Kill switches are persisted and protective exits keep running.
- Backtests execute on the next bar's open and include fees, slippage and spread. They are
  compared with a passive benchmark over the same period, and failed experiments are kept.
- Financial values use Decimal/NUMERIC, and all times are UTC.
- The universe comes from exchange metadata; there is no hard-coded coin list.

## C. What did not match (now corrected unless noted in F/I)

- Health and status were partly assumed: the dashboard said "healthy", `/market/context` said
  liquidity `STRONG`, and Integrations "test connection" only checked configuration.
- Five scheduler jobs reported success without doing real work.
- `DEMO_MODE` defaulted to `true`, so synthetic candles, a seeded strategy with fabricated PASS
  validation and a fabricated FOMC event were the default experience.
- Decision confidence was an arbitrary 45/35/20 score, and there was no structured trade
  rationale.
- There was no FRED integration, and CryptoPanic was present although it is out of scope.
- There was no venue separation, and no testnet path.
- There were look-ahead windows in lineage (+1 h) and in the scanner (+24 h) — see R.

## F. Fixed in this pass

- **Truthful integrations** (`app/integrations/health.py`): real checks, persisted with endpoint,
  latency, status and redacted detail. States are HEALTHY/DEGRADED/UNAVAILABLE/STALE/
  NOT_CONFIGURED/NOT_VERIFIED. A model that is listed but cannot generate counts as unavailable.
  Transient 429/5xx responses get one retry. Plan-restricted endpoints show as
  `NOT_AVAILABLE_ON_PLAN`.
- **Secret handling**: log/exception redaction of configured secret values and credential query
  parameters. The httpx/httpcore loggers are raised to WARNING because they print full URLs,
  including `token=` and `api_key=`.
- **FRED** (`app/integrations/fred.py`): each vintage is stored with observation date, release
  (`realtime_start`), `available_at`, `retrieved_at`, units and title. Ingestion is idempotent,
  and `macro_as_of(t)` is point-in-time.
- **Evidence reasoner** (`app/trading/reasoning.py`) with the structured decision object stored in
  `Decision.rationale`:
  - asset, direction, time, strategy/version/hash, regime (setup + context), thesis
  - supporting / contradicting / blocking evidence, invalidation, horizon
  - news/macro/historical context, data sources, risk-config fingerprint
  - plan: entry reference, stop, target, R:R, quantity, notional, risk amount/% and venue
  - AI review
- **AI trade review** (`off/advisory/veto`): the AI may only turn a TRADE into NO TRADE. If the AI
  is unavailable, that is recorded and no text is fabricated.
- **Risk gates** from `MarketFacts`: stale or missing candles, no liquidity evidence, ineligible
  asset, spread too wide or unknown, insufficient liquidity, reward:risk below minimum, and
  `EXECUTION_DISABLED`.
- **Execution modes** `paper | testnet | live_disabled`; `live` is rejected. Venue-scoped
  positions and portfolio. The Binance Spot Testnet adapter:
  - uses a constant host and separate keys
  - uses idempotent client order IDs
  - records rejections without opening positions
  - performs real testnet SELLs for exits (a failure is recorded as `EXIT_FAILED` and the
    position stays open)
  - reconciles after restart without placing orders
- **Jobs**: purpose, schedule, retry policy, duration and redacted errors. News ingestion now
  fails when every source fails. New jobs: `macro_ingestion`, `provider_verification`, and
  `testnet_reconciliation` (testnet mode only). Registry at `/api/v1/system/jobs/registry`.
- **Frontend**:
  - Health page built from `/health` components, with a "Verify providers now" button.
  - Integrations page shows real check tables.
  - Decision monitor shows the rationale (blocking/contradicting/supporting evidence, plan, AI
    review).
  - Mode banner shows data mode + execution mode.
  - Intelligence and Safety pages use the new provider states.
- **Migration `0006`**: provider_checks, macro_observations, venue columns, decision rationale
  and event availability times, with backfill. Upgrading a populated 0004 database left the
  existing data unchanged, and `alembic check` is clean.

- Existing market events lacked `available_at`; they were backfilled from
  `published_at`/`event_at`.
- The paper portfolio from before 0006 was tagged `venue=paper`.
- The synthetic `paper` exchange data is now isolated to DEMO_MODE, but demo rows in an old

## R. Look-ahead and backtesting

- **Fixed:**
  - Decision lineage accepted events up to +1 h after the decision.
  - The scanner accepted events up to +24 h after the candle.
  - Both now use `knowable_at ≤ decision time`.
- **Added:**
  - Regime context only from candles closed before the decision.
  - Scheduled macro events only if retrieved before the decision.
  - FRED values only from vintages public at the time.
- **Verified OK:**
  - Signals are computed on the closed bar and executed at the next open.
  - Stop checks come before target checks.
  - Costs are applied on both legs, and the benchmark covers the same period.
- **Residual:**
  - `ResearchAnalyzer` "swing highs" use the next bar (`shift(-1)`). The output is descriptive
    and is never used as a signal.
  - Declarative strategies have no fitted parameters, so walk-forward measures stability rather
    than refitting. Hypotheses chosen after looking at history are handled through trial
    counting and deflated Sharpe, not by the walk-forward itself.
